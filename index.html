Profile

From curious about the screen to building what runs behind it.

22-year-old full-stack web developer and Senior Software Engineering student at KFUPM. Toward the end of high school I got curious about how websites actually work — what's behind the screen. At university that curiosity turned into learning to code, and in November 2023 I committed to it properly by joining a web development bootcamp. Since then my focus has been building modern, flexible websites that hold up in production — and, more recently, understanding the security layer that protects them. When I'm not at the computer I'm scuba diving, at the gym, reading, watching series, or with friends.
